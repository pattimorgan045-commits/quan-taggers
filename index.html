Welcome to Quan Taggers

Quan Taggers is a multiplayer virtual reality tag game focused on physical movement, climbing, chasing, and escaping other players.

Move through the environment, use your surroundings, and play tag with friends in VR.

About

Quan Taggers is designed for Meta Quest virtual reality headsets. Features and gameplay may change as development continues.

Support

For questions, bug reports, or support, contact the developer through the support/community channel where you received the game.
