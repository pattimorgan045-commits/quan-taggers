Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Quan Taggers handles information when you use the game.

Information collected

Quan Taggers is designed to collect only information that is necessary for the game to function. Depending on the features enabled in the current version, this may include gameplay, networking, or account information provided by the platform or required for multiplayer functionality.

Microphone

If voice chat is enabled, Quan Taggers may access the device microphone while the game is in use so players can communicate with one another. Microphone audio is not used for purposes unrelated to the game's communication features.

Third parties

Quan Taggers may use platform or networking services required to provide multiplayer functionality. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies.

Children

Quan Taggers does not knowingly request personal information from children beyond information needed for the platform and game to operate. Parents or guardians can contact the developer with privacy questions.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through the support/community channel where you received the game.
